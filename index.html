Getting started

Join in three steps

You need the same modpack as the server. Everything else (the economy, towns, nations) is built into the server, so there's nothing extra to install.

1

Get the Technic Launcher

Download it from technicpack.net, sign in with your Minecraft account, and search the modpacks for Tekkit 2.

2

Install Tekkit 2 v1.2.6

Pick version 1.2.6 so it matches the server, then press Play and let it finish installing. We recommend giving it 6 GB of RAM or more in the launcher settings.

3

Add the server

Multiplayer → Add Server → address yomikeysmp.modrinth.gg. Join, and you'll land on the spawn deck facing the Y.

Find your thing

Ways to play

There's no single path. Every role earns Forge Coins and matters to everyone else.

Miner

Dig up uranium, iridium and the other mined-only materials and sell them at the Exchange for real money.

Shopkeeper

Rent a lot in Forge City, stock chest shops, and earn while you're offline. Buyers find you with /store.

Hauler

Take freight jobs between stations, deliver on time and get paid. /freight list

Engineer

Build reactors, power grids, ME systems and factories, for yourself or for hire.

Rail builder

Lay the lines that connect towns. Nations and companies pay for good rail.

Trader

Watch the Exchange board, buy what's cheap, sell what's short, and flip auctions.

Founder

Start a company, sell shares, hire players and pay dividends. /co new

Leader

Run a town, lead a nation, sit as a judge or negotiate treaties as a diplomat.

          Economy

          Forge Coins

          Every player has a coin balance on the server. Coins are earned by selling to the Exchange or to other players, and spent in shops, the auction house, rent, town upkeep and taxes.

          The Exchange trades 20+ materials, and the list grows. Prices move with its stock: flood it with iron and iron gets cheap, run it dry and sellers get paid more. It only sells what players have sold to it, so nothing comes from thin air.

          ProjectE is mined-only for rare goods. Uranium, iridium, steel, desh, titanium, nether stars and dark/red matter have no EMC, so they have to be mined or made. That's what gives mining towns their value.

          /bal/pay/exchange/exchange list/sell all/store/ah
          Run a shop

          Signs on chests

          Put a chest in the Market Hall, your shop lot or your town, then place a sign on it. Line 1 is [Shop] to sell or [Buy] to buy, line 2 is the amount, and line 3 is the price. Right-click the sign to activate it.

          Buyers pay straight into your balance, even while you're offline. Every active shop shows up in /store, so people can find you.

          Selling something rare? List it on the auction house with /ah sell <price> or start a bidding war with /ah auction <start> [hours].

          [Shop]Sell 16120 FCkickface102
          [Buy]Buying 64300 FCIronholdCo

          Example signs after activation. A [Buy] sign pays sellers from the owner's balance.

          Trade in one menu

          The Exchange menu

          Inside the Exchange, type /exchange or right-click a terminal. Every item opens in one chest-style menu, with its price, the Exchange's stock and how many you're carrying in the tooltip.

          Left-click buys one and shift-click buys 16. Right-click sells one and shift-right-click sells all you carry. The hopper sells everything the Exchange buys in one go. It works with the normal Tekkit client, nothing to install.

          Want something new traded? Hold it and type /exchange request. Staff see a most-wanted list with a suggested price, and when an item gets listed the News Wire announces it.

          Towns

          Claim your land

          Found a town at least 160 blocks from spawn with /t new <name>. Your chunk becomes the town's home, and you can expand one chunk at a time with /t claim.

          Town land is protected. Outsiders can't build, break, open chests or machines, or blow anything up. Quarries and turtles keep working. PvP is off unless your town turns it on.

          Towns have their own bank. Mayors set a daily tax, and the town pays daily upkeep. If the bank runs dry, the town loses its newest chunk each day. Keep it funded.

          Rail & logistics

          Stations, rail and freight

          Towns build stations. Players lay the track. Ride a minecart from one station to another and the link is added to the network map automatically. Then people pay each other to move goods along it.

          /station list/station links/station route A B/freight list/freight help/station collect
          Business

          Companies

          Start a mining outfit, a power company or a rail line with /co new <name>. You become CEO and own all 1,000 shares.

          Hire staff, promote managers and pay salaries from the company bank. Sell stakes by transferring shares, then pay everyone at once with /co dividend. The payout is split by how many shares each person holds.

          /co help/co shares/co dividend/co pay

          Diplomacy

          Treaties are real documents

          A leader drafts a treaty with /treaty draft <nation> <type> <terms>. The other leader gets Sign and Reject buttons. Once it's signed, both leaders receive an official written book, and the treaty is posted on the Registry boards in the Hall of Nations.

          Treaties do things. An alliance stops allies hurting each other and blocks war between them. A trade treaty removes tariffs. A non-aggression pact forbids declaring war. A peace treaty ends one.

          Leaders can set tariffs on foreign shoppers, impose sanctions that block trade, and vote in the Grand Council, where a passed SANCTION <nation> resolution binds everyone.

          Server rules

          Keep it fun for everyone

          1. Be decent

          No harassment, slurs, hate speech or spam. Keep chat, signs, names and builds PG-13.

          2. No griefing or stealing

          Don't break, take or change anything that isn't yours, claimed or not. Every block change is logged and can be rolled back.

          3. No cheating

          No hacked clients, x-ray, duping or exploiting bugs. Found a bug? Tell staff with /report.

          4. No lag machines

          No giant mob farms, item spam or chunk-loading empty land.

          5. Reactors and explosives

          Only on your own claimed land, away from other people. No nukes.

          6. PvP

          Allowed in the wilderness. Off in Forge City, and off in towns unless the town turns it on. Wars follow /war rules.

          7. Fair deals

          Scamming is a crime here. Victims can take you to /court.

          8. Give others room

          Don't claim or build right up against another town without asking.

          9. The rail network

          Main lines are two tracks with signals. Don't park carts on main lines or cut someone else's line.

          10. One account each

          No alts to farm starter coins or swing elections.

          11. Staff and courts

          Staff and judge decisions stand. Disagree? Use /report or talk to staff privately.

          Breaking rules: warning, then mute or jail, then kick, then ban. Serious griefing or cheating can mean an instant ban. Read them in game with /rules.

          Is it free? Do I need to install anything extra?

          It's free. You only need the Tekkit 2 modpack (v1.2.6) from the Technic Launcher. Everything else, from the economy to the screens, runs on the server.

          How do I become staff?

          Play for at least 2 hours, then type /apply in game and answer six short questions. An admin reviews every application and you'll get a message either way.

          How do I make money fast?

          Mine and sell to the Exchange early. Prices for scarce goods like uranium start high and drop as players sell. After that, run a shop, take freight jobs, or work for a company.

          Why can't I EMC-condense uranium or nether stars?

          Those items are mined-only on this server so that mining towns and trade matter. Common items still work with ProjectE as normal.

          Can I lose my town?

          Only if the town bank can't pay daily upkeep. You lose the newest chunk each day it's unpaid, and the town falls after 3 unpaid days with only the home chunk left. Deposit with /t deposit.

          Is my stuff safe in Forge City?

          Yes. Spawn is protected and mob-free. Your personal vault at the Vault Bank and anything in your claimed pod or lot can only be touched by you and people you trust.

          How do new items get added to the Exchange?

          Hold the item and type /exchange request. Trying to /sell something unlisted counts as demand too. Staff list the most-wanted items, and the News Wire announces each new one.

          Something got griefed. What do I do?

          Don't rebuild it yourself. Type /report with what happened. Staff can see exactly who changed every block and roll it back.

          What happens to my items if the auction doesn't sell?

          They go to your claim box. Type /ah claim any time to collect them.
