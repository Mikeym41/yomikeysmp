Rail & freight

Stations

  • Mayors build a station on town land with /station create <name> (200 FC from the town bank).
  • Ride a minecart from one station to another and the link is added to the network automatically.
  • /station list, /station route <from> <to>, and /station collect to pick up deliveries.

Freight jobs

  • /freight list shows open jobs. Take one with /freight accept <#> and finish with /freight deliver <#>.
  • Post a supply job to pay someone to bring items: /freight supply <station> <amount> <reward>.
  • Post a haul job to have your cargo moved: /freight haul <to station> <reward> <collateral>. The hauler's deposit is yours if they never deliver.

Rules for the shared network

  • Main lines are two tracks, one each direction. No single track running both ways.
  • Use Railcraft block signals and locking tracks so carts wait for a clear section.
  • Put stations on side tracks off the main line, with a buffer stop at the end.
  • Don't park carts on a main line, and ask before connecting to or changing someone else's line.
